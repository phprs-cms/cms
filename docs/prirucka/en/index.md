# phpRS manual

phpRS is a publishing system for online magazines, newspapers and blogs. This manual takes you from installation to day-to-day operation.

It is written for two kinds of readers. **The newsroom** – people who write, edit and publish – will find what they need in the chapters on writing and editorial work.
**The site administrator** – the person who installed the system and looks after it – starts with the chapters Getting started and Operations.

## Chapters

- **Getting started** – hosting requirements, installation, first steps after installation, updates and moving the site elsewhere.
- **Operations** – mail, backups, background jobs, System status, nginx, security and troubleshooting.
- *Writing, Newsroom, Appearance, Readers and revenue, Language versions, SEO and AI, For developers* – in preparation.

## How the manual is written

Names of screens and buttons are in **bold** and match the English administration interface. A path in the administration is written with arrows,
for example **Settings → Mail**. Addresses and file names look `like this`.

The manual describes the latest released version of the system. You will find your version number in the administration at the bottom of the **Dashboard**
and in **Settings → Backups and updates**.
